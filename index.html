Overview

Building world models that act, plan, and adapt.

World models infer and predict real-world dynamics by modeling the external environment, and have become a cornerstone of embodied artificial intelligence. They have powered recent progress in decision-making and planning for interactive agents.

This workshop brings together researchers working across generative modeling, reinforcement learning, computer vision, language, and robotics to explore the next generation of embodied world models. The goal is to move beyond passive prediction toward active, goal-directed interaction with physical and simulated worlds.

Topics

Areas of interest

Model-based reinforcement learning

Planning, sample efficiency, long-horizon credit assignment, and scalable control.

Simulation and real-world physics

Bridging sim-to-real gaps through grounded dynamics, uncertainty, and feedback.

Interactive scene generation

Action-conditioned video, controllable environments, and evaluation for downstream tasks.

Video-language-action models

Representations that unify perception, language, action, and world knowledge.

Robotics and autonomy

Manipulation, navigation, autonomous driving, embodied QA, and open-world games.

Evaluation and benchmarks

Metrics for temporal consistency, physical plausibility, controllability, and utility.

Call For Papers

Submission information

We invite original research, position papers, and benchmark papers related to physically grounded world models for embodied agents. Submissions may include theoretical foundations, algorithmic advances, systems, applications, or thoughtful opinion pieces.

  • Opinion papers: up to 4 pages, unlimited references.
  • Research papers: 4 to 9 pages, unlimited references and appendices.
  • Accepted papers will be presented in a poster session.
  • Submissions are reviewed in a double-blind process.
Submit via OpenReview
