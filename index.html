Overview

About the workshop

This workshop explores how machine learning can support athlete performance, safety, fairness, and inclusive participation while remaining scientifically robust, interpretable, reproducible, and useful for real-world decision-making.

We welcome work that combines methodological advances with careful evaluation, domain grounding, uncertainty communication, and responsible deployment in sport settings.

Topics of interest

Indicative themes

Inclusive and Paralympic sport

Data-scarce, athlete-centred methods for classification research, biomechanics, performance, and safety.

Multimodal sports intelligence

Combining tracking data, video, wearables, context, and expert knowledge for decision support.

Team and invasion sports

Understanding tactics, movement, uncertainty, and coordinated decision-making in dynamic play.

Trustworthy evaluation

Benchmarking, reproducibility, uncertainty quantification, generalisation, and responsible deployment.
